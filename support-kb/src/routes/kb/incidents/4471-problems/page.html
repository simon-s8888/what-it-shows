The three problems

parser: the shared parser reads every date month first. Every tenant in #4471 writes the day first. A date like 7 April comes out as 4 July. This is the bug #4471 names, and the fix queued for it.

hint: booking-v3 prints MM/DD/YYYY under its date field. Tenants on booking-v3 write the day first, so the form reads the date day first. Their people type what the hint says, month first. The date comes out swapped through the form even after the parser is fixed. Only reports through the form channel at booking-v3 tenants.

one-day: dates shift by one day, not by a swap. These are the reports the desk already noted as a one-day move (desk-notes.json). Their bodies describe a meeting one day early or late, a different day in Lisbon and in Toronto, an invite that arrived before the booking, one month (June) off by one day. That is a time zone or daylight-saving bug, not a date order bug. The parser fix does not touch it.
